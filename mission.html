Our Mission & Vision

Glorifying God Through Music and Ministry

🎵 Musical Excellence

We strive to achieve the highest standards of choral performance.

✝️ Spiritual Formation

Beyond music, we are committed to nurturing the spiritual growth of our members.

🤝 Community Building

We create a welcoming family where members support each other.

📚 Youth Development

We are dedicated to mentoring young people.

🌍 Community Outreach

Through performances and ministry, we reach out to the broader community.

🌟 Legacy Building

We preserve and pass on our rich heritage while embracing innovation.

Our Vision

To be a leading choir in Rwanda and beyond.
