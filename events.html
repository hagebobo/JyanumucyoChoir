📅 October 11-18, 2026

JYANUMUCYO WEEK 2026

🙏 Theme: "JYANUMUCYO YACU ISHEMA RYA KRISTO"

A week dedicated to the JYANUMUCYO Choir, featuring worship, health screening, community outreach, and acts of love.

📍 Location: Huye & Nyamagabe Districts

📋 Daily Program:

  • 🔹 Oct 11: Prison Ministry at Nyamagabe - Visiting and ministering to inmates
  • 🔹 Oct 12: "Tubasange Iwabo" Program - Visiting elderly members in Tumba, Cyarwa, Ngoma, Mbazi
  • 🔹 Oct 13: Family Ministry - Home visits and evangelism
  • 🔹 Oct 14: Orphan Outreach at Mubumbano/Mukura
  • 🔹 Oct 15-17: Free Health Screening - Non-communicable diseases (NCDs) and mental health awareness at the Cathedral
  • 🔹 Oct 17: Hospital Visitation - Supporting patients at medical facilities
  • 🔹 Oct 18: Grand Closing Celebration - Thanksgiving service with health screening and welcoming guests
